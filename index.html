About

I'm Basu Saha, a book-cover designer and illustrator specialising in concept-driven cover design, original illustration and typography.

I started drawing when I was just three, and even while completing my Physics Honours degree, I never stopped drawing. Later, I introduced myself to graphic design and typography through books and online courses, and realized I could finally combine my love for illustration with my habit of reading.

Today, I design book covers for authors to help their stories reach the people who need them.

Process
01

Discovery

If the manuscript is ready, I start by reading the book. If it is still in progress, I send a detailed questionnaire covering the synopsis, underlying themes, characters and their relationships, the environment, key visual cues, and the technical specifications required for production.

02

Genre Research

Once I understand the book, I study its genre thoroughly to see what resonates with readers, what feels repetitive, and how to make the cover fit its category while remaining distinct.

03

The Brief

Together with the author, I put together a focused design brief. Having a clear brief keeps the vision sharp so we are both aligned on the tone and direction before any design work begins.

04

Cover Concepts

Rather than presenting abstract mood boards that can be difficult to visualize (I keep visual direction studies strictly for my own internal reference), I move straight into exploring several initial front cover concepts. You can see tangible directions, choose the one that fits best, and we refine it together.

05

Full Wrap & Print Files

After the front cover is finalized, I design the spine and back cover to complete the full wrap, then prepare the final files ready for print and digital publishing.

06

Handcrafted, Zero AI

Every illustration is drawn by hand in Procreate on my iPad, and all typography, layout, and print preparation are built using Affinity or Adobe design tools. When a cover uses photography or archival imagery, I source it from public domain collections and verify each license before using it. I do not use generative AI or AI generated artwork anywhere in my cover design process.
