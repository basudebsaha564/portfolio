Process
01

Discovery

First, I start by understanding the project and the book. I'll give you a questionnaire doc to learn all the technical and literary details about the book. And if time and resources allow, I can also read the entire book to understand it from a reader's perspective, and get all the necessary literary details for our design process.

02

Research

In this phase, I research on the genre, the audience, and trends. This step is crucial as my aim is not only to make a beautiful cover that stands out, but also to clearly signal the genre to attract the right audience.

03

The Brief

In this step, I'll write down a clear design brief with you, which will help us understand and decide what we want to communicate using the cover. For example, a cover can communicate many things, but not all. It can communicate characters, settings, themes, metaphors, emotions, and more.

04

Design Direction

In this step, we finalize the design direction based on everything we have learned so far. Do we want it to be image-led, illustrated, conceptual, or type-led? We decide all that here. This step is to make sure in the later stages, we both can be on the same page.

05

The Design

Now it's time to design the concepts and iterate with feedback.

06

Full Wrap and Print Files

Once the front cover is ready, I design the spine and back. After your approval, I finally create all the deliverables (as discussed in our agreement) and print-ready files.

About

Hi, I am Basu Saha, a book cover designer and illustrator from India.

I started my drawing and painting journey when I was about 3 years old. My mother got me admitted to a local drawing school, where I had my fine arts training for 8 years. There, I got the opportunity to work with different mediums like watercolour, oil pastels, pencil colours, and even oil paints. That's also why I like experimenting with different mediums, textures, and finishes in my work, even though my primary medium is digital.

In college, I chose physics as my honours subject and eventually graduated in physics, but I always continued my drawing practice at home. After graduation, I decided to pursue graphic design and taught myself for a few years while also taking some commercial projects here and there.

Later, I figured out I could also apply my design skills to book covers. And honestly, since childhood, I have always loved reading books in various genres, from literary fiction and short story collections to nonfiction. I also loved collecting books with beautiful artwork. So, I am really grateful that I am designing real book covers now!
